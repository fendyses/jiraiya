### Key Insights & Learning

- **The status column is destructive.** An `ApplicationStatusLog::created` boot hook rewrites `applications.application_status_id` on every transition. 19 applications currently show "Executed Document"; **143 have ever reached it**. The naive `!= 19` query misclassifies ~124 executed documents as outstanding. Everything must be resolved from history.
- **Literal "not yet executed" is useless**: 1,383 of 1,402 rows, 524 of them terminal. The honest in-flight number is **853**.
- **Guessed slugs fail silently.** Four of five terminal slugs I wrote didn't exist — status 1 "Application Withdrawn" has the slug `new`. `whereIn` just matched fewer rows and inflated Outstanding by 28. Added a `Log::warning` guard and a `puu:verify` check.
- **The data is starkly bimodal.** Closed vetting cycles: median **0** working days (52% finish same day, raw gaps of 2 minutes to 9 hours). Open cycles: median **1,043** working days, 70 of 74 running since 2022. Those aren't late — they're abandoned. Split "breached" from "abandoned" (>90 wd, excluded from averages): breached went 99 → 10 and team SLA health 68% → 98.6%. Without the split, compliance is permanently pinned low by 2022 records.
- **Dual-role users broke the RBAC assumption.** I had written "nobody holds both tiers." Wrong: **292 users hold >1 role and 7 of 13 vetters also hold Admin.** They would have opened "My Performance" and seen all 1,402 documents under a heading saying "assigned to me". Fixed with intent-based scoping (`visibleTo($user, $selfOnly)`).
- **Accountability isn't the role.** 8 people have assigned vetters but only 4 hold Legal Advisor — and one person did 731 of 789. Grouping had to follow who actually acted.
- **Cross-stage reconciliation caught a real bug.** Stage 1 said 81 awaiting, stage 2 said 64 not-started. The 17-document gap: `assigned_to` set with no status-4 log, so the assignment clock would tick forever on work already assigned. Now `unmeasurable`; both stages agree at 64, and the reconciliation is a permanent check.
- **Two units of "day" silently disagreed.** `working_days` counted date boundaries, `calendar_days` used `diffInDays` (24h periods), so a 21-hour overnight gap rendered "1 working day · 0 cal" on 94 of 853 documents. Both now count boundaries.
- **Blade indentation cost 65% of a page.** Work Monitoring was 2,082,831 bytes; tightening the row markup cut it to 724,276 with identical output. Also found `deferRender: true` was a no-op on a DOM-sourced table and removed it.
